A free cozy pixel cafe game for your browser

Elevator Music is a relaxing pixel-art game you can play free in your browser, no download. Dress your character, step out into a late-night city at 11:42 PM, and ride the elevator between music floors: jazz, pop, indie, R&B, rap, and your own floor for a lofi or chill playlist.

When you are ready to settle in, sit down in the cafe and start the study timer. It is a lofi study game for working, studying or winding down.

How to play: arrow keys or A and D to walk, Space to talk, pick things up and sit down. Sign in to Spotify for the full music experience.

Frequently asked questions

Is Elevator Music Game free to play?

Yes. Elevator Music is completely free and runs in your browser, so there is nothing to download or install.

What is Elevator Music Game?

It is a cozy pixel-art browser game. You dress your character, wander a late-night city at 11:42 PM, ride the elevator between floors with different music, and settle into a cafe.

Does it work as a lofi study timer?

Yes. Sit down in the cafe and start the built-in study timer. It starts at 25 minutes and you can adjust it in 5-minute steps, so you can study or work with lofi or chill music in the background.

Do I need Spotify to play?

You can play without it, but signing in to Spotify gives you the full music experience, with a different playlist on each floor.

What music floors are there?

Jazz, pop, indie, R&B and rap, plus your own floor where you can listen to a lofi or chill playlist.

How do I play?

Use the arrow keys or A and D to walk. Press Space to talk, pick things up and sit down.
